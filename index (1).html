What is a 2-minute timer good for?

Teachers, parents and students use a 2-minute timer for two-minute tooth brushing, fast clean-ups, breathing breaks and short speaking turns. The shrinking colored wedge shows how much time is left at a glance, which helps with time blindness, ADHD and transitions, especially for kids who can’t read a digital clock yet.

Does it keep time in the background?

Yes. The countdown is based on the actual clock time, so it stays accurate if you switch tabs or present slides. Keep this tab open so the sound can play when time is up. Press the Space bar to start or pause.
